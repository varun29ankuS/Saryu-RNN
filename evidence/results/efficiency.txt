THE FIRST RESULT IN THIS PROJECT ON THE AXIS THE PROJECT IS ABOUT -- AND ONE SEED OF IT.
================================================================================
Saryu reaches bpc 3.0 in 409,600 tokens where a parameter-matched transformer needs
2,252,800. That is 5.5x fewer tokens, and because the token gap is larger than the
2.8x per-step cost, it is ALSO 2.5x less wall-clock (0.06 h against 0.15 h). Both
halves of "cheap intelligence" move in the same direction for the first time. Every
prior result in this repository measured recall capacity on synthetic MQAR, which is
a capability axis; 94 of 110 results files have no run behind them and all of those
are MQAR. This one is on the goal.

AND THIS SESSION'S TWO ADDITIONS MADE IT WORSE. P3 is falsified in the direction that
costs us: saryu-plain reaches bpc 3.0 in 204,800 tokens and bpc 2.5 in 819,200, against
409,600 and 1,024,000 with the matrix memory and gate lower bound switched on. Both were
built for recall capacity, both were measured to work there, and on language modelling
they are a 2x efficiency PENALTY. That is a genuine finding and it was not the one being
looked for: the components that fix MQAR are not the components that make this model
learn from less text.

WHAT THIS IS NOT, and the list is long enough that nothing here should be quoted as a
headline yet:

  ONE SEED. Every arm in this project has come back bimodal -- both_fixes had seeds of
  0.398 and 0.992 on the same configuration. A single seed is a lottery ticket, and the
  rule added to CLAIMS.md after the third voided experiment applies here too.

  ONE LEARNING RATE for four architectures. stability_margin.txt measured different
  architectures peaking at different rates (DeltaNet best at 3e-3). Giving every arm
  1e-3, with no warmup and no schedule, is a confound that could plausibly account for
  the whole transformer gap on its own.

  A SMALL-DATA REGIME. 3.07M tokens of enwik8 at ctx 128. The literature's position is
  that linear attention is competitive or better at small scale and falls behind as data
  grows, so this is the regime where our result is LEAST surprising and least predictive.

  NOTHING CONVERGED. The transformer was still falling when stopped (2.895 -> 2.821 ->
  2.751) and so was saryu-plain (2.189). Tokens-to-threshold at a threshold both arms
  reached is a fair comparison; the ORDERING at a larger budget is not established.

  THE GRU ARM IS THE BARE VERSION. It ran before the pre-norm/residual fix, so its 3.051
  is a property of a known-weak baseline, and P2 is unresolved rather than answered.

WHAT WOULD MAKE IT A CLAIM: three seeds, a per-arm learning-rate sweep, and a budget that
reaches convergence. Until then this is one encouraging seed, reported in full.
