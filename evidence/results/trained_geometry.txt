THE GEOMETRY OF THE TRAINED MODEL -- measured, not constructed
==============================================================

WHY. Nine mechanisms this session were shown to work BY CONSTRUCTION and none was shown to survive
training. Almost all our geometry describes what the architecture COULD represent. This file is the
other kind: measurements on the shipped 25M checkpoint and real enwik8, with no hand-built vectors
anywhere.

    checkpoint  d = 744, H = 8, dh = 93, nh = 2, 4 layers, vocab 481

1. BETA IS PINNED AT A PURE REFLECTION -- so no volume is lost, and my hypothesis was wrong.
   det H(u,beta) = 1 - beta, so the transport preserves volume only at beta = 0 or 2, and beta = 1
   is exactly SINGULAR (I - u u^T is a projection that destroys a dimension permanently). The
   worry was that a trained model drifts into the contracting middle and quietly loses rank.

      layer 0-1   beta median 2.000, min 1.998
      layer 2     beta median 1.998, min 1.947
      layer 3     beta median 1.988, min 1.964
      fraction with |1-beta| < 0.2 (near singular): 0.000 everywhere

   The model kept pure reflections and never approaches the singularity. b_proj weight norms grew
   from 0 at init to 4.11/4.95/5.09/5.43, so input dependence WAS learned -- beta varies with the
   token while staying near 2. Volume-loss hypothesis: DEAD.

2. THE TRANSPORT IS RANK 2 IN A 93-DIMENSIONAL HEAD. This is the thing we overlooked.
   Two reflections rotate ONE 2-plane out of 93. The whole project has called this "the transport"
   without noting how little of the state it moves:

      tr(R)/dh = 1 - 2*nh/dh = 1 - 4/93 = 0.957

   composition.txt measured the chaining threshold on exactly this quantity: at tr/d = 0.984
   chaining is indistinguishable from commutative binding (0.496 at k=2), at 0.938 it is partial
   (0.716), and only by 0.750 does it match a permutation. The 25M model sits at 0.957 -- in the
   failing band. Reaching the working band needs nh ~ dh/2 ~ 46 reflections per head against the 2
   it has.

   CORRECTION TO MY OWN CLAIM. An hour before this I said the model's heads were dh=16 with nh=2,
   giving tr/dh = 0.750 and landing in the working band. That was the small TEST model (d=128).
   The real checkpoint is dh=93 and lands in the failing band.

   DESIGN RULE, and it runs against normal practice: dh grows with model width while nh stays
   pinned at 2, so LARGER MODELS GET MONOTONICALLY WORSE at directed relation. The quantity to
   hold fixed is the RATIO nh/dh. Every nh sweep in this project varied nh with dh held constant
   and so never saw the ratio.

3. THE COST ASYMMETRY THAT SETTLES THE DESIGN.
   Direction from reflections costs O(nh*dh) per head, and nh ~ dh/2 makes that dh^2/2 -- exactly
   what a matrix state costs. Direction from a PERMUTATION costs O(d) and, per composition.txt,
   buys the same six free hops. So reflections are the expensive way to purchase something a
   permutation gives away, and the cheap route to directed relational memory is convolution
   binding plus a permutation role.

4. THE STATE IS NOT SPARSE. Participation ratio (sum lam)^2 / sum lam^2 of the per-head state
   covariance over real text, out of dh = 93:

      layer 0   58.3 53.0 58.9 59.6 56.7 58.5 58.9 60.1    mean 58.0
      layer 1   63.8 61.9 60.2 63.0 61.8 64.2 62.0 60.0    mean 62.1
      layer 2   62.6 59.2 58.3 60.9 60.7 61.0 61.2 57.3    mean 60.1
      layer 3   44.0 25.9 33.8 47.0 45.7 45.6 37.5 35.7    mean 39.4

   60-67% of every head is carrying variance in layers 0-2. The hypothesis that capacity failures
   are explained by the model using a handful of its dimensions is FALSE. Layer 3 compresses to
   ~39, which is what a readout layer should do.

5. THE TIMESCALE LADDER EMERGED BY ITSELF, IN THE DEEPER LAYERS. Gate retention 1/g per head:

      layer 0   12 13 13 13 14 14 15 16
      layer 1   11 12 13 13 14 14 14 17
      layer 2   14 14 15 16 19 20 86 132
      layer 3   15 19 22 23 25 28 48 49

   Three separate attempts to install this by hand (timescales, freeze_gate_bias, gate_ceiling)
   all collapsed to 2-3 tokens within 1000 steps (gate_timescales.txt). Training produced it
   unaided at depth, with heads at 86 and 132 tokens in layer 2. Where we intervened it died;
   where we left it alone it appeared.

6. EFFECTIVE CONTEXT SATURATES NEAR 256 CHARACTERS. Loss at the final position with only the last
   k characters visible, 512 independent samples per k:

      k        loss      SE    vs full
      4      1.6014  0.0694    +0.6509
      8      1.2190  0.0653    +0.2684
      16     1.0611  0.0608    +0.1106
      32     0.9844  0.0584    +0.0339
      64     0.9654  0.0589    +0.0149
      128    0.9554  0.0589    +0.0049
      256    0.9511  0.0589    +0.0006
      512    0.9505  0.0589    +0.0000

   Paired over the same samples, k=16 is worse than full context by +0.1106 +- 0.0185, t = 5.98 --
   so the model really does use long context. But 256 -> 512 is worth +0.0006. The documented
   "stops using context at about 512 characters" is generous; the measured saturation is ~256.

   MY OWN FIRST READING OF THIS WAS NOISE. With 16 samples at one position the k=8 loss came out
   equal to full and k=16 came out BETTER, which would have said the effective context was eight
   characters. The spread across k was 0.997-1.084 against a per-sample SE far larger than the
   differences. Recorded because it is the fourth time in this session that an under-powered
   measurement produced a dramatic and false reading.

OPEN / NOT CLAIMED
  - One checkpoint. Nothing here is a scaling law, including the nh/dh rule, which is a prediction
    from the construction in composition.txt applied to this model's numbers, not a trained result.
  - Participation ratio measures variance, not information. A head could spread variance over 60
    directions and still carry little task-relevant content in them.
  - The effective-context measurement truncates the input, which also removes the conv's left
    context; at k = 4 that confounds the two. It should not matter by k = 32.
  - The emergent timescale ladder is a description of one trained model, not evidence that it is
    what makes the model work.
