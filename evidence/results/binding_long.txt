P1 HOLDS for adamw, and decisively: binding is not merely reachable, it goes to 0.94. The
whole session's architecture comparisons were run at 1500-3000 steps, i.e. inside the first
third of the plateau, which is why every arm looked identical.

P2 FAILS. The prediction was that a preconditioner rescaling stiff directions would escape
SOONER. This one never escaped at all.

THE CONFOUND. Muon's final loss is 0.833 against adamw's 0.073. It is not that muon learned
the set and stalled before binding -- it learned everything worse. A fair test of the
preconditioner needs muon at a learning rate where its LOSS is competitive, and this run
does not provide that: lr was fixed at 1e-3 for both, with a sqrt(max(shape)) scale factor
chosen here rather than taken from the Muon literature. So the supported claim is

  "this muon configuration never escaped"      NOT     "preconditioning does not help"

and the conditioning hypothesis is not yet properly tested. An lr sweep for muon follows.
