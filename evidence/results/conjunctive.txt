READ -- A DOUBLE DISSOCIATION, AND A CONJUNCTION THAT IS NOT YET INTERPRETABLE

WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, and it is the strongest form of evidence for the design principle.
Ablating one path AT THE QUERY POSITION on the two single-axis tasks:

    track-only (S_3)    kill transport: 1.000 -> 0.348 (chance 0.333)   kill memory: 1.000 -> 1.000
    recall-only         kill transport: 1.000 -> 1.000/0.969            kill memory: 1.000 -> 0.828
    MQAR (state_volume) kill transport: 0.926 -> 0.906                  kill memory: 0.926 -> 0.086

Each path is causally NECESSARY for exactly the capability it was designed for and causally
IRRELEVANT for the other. The transport carries state tracking and contributes nothing to recall;
the memory carries recall and contributes nothing to tracking. That is a double dissociation, and
it is what "a recurrence that conflates tracking with storage gets neither" predicts.

WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED. Composition. The conjunctive task sits at 0.39-0.44 against chance 0.333
with or without the memory, so its ablations measure nothing -- you cannot ablate a capability the
model never acquired. P1 is falsified as stated (memory=True does not beat memory=False; it is
marginally worse), but the falsification is uninformative while the task is unlearned.

TWO DESIGN ERRORS IN THIS EXPERIMENT, recorded because they bound what it can show.

  1. THE STORAGE HALF IS TRIVIAL. recall-only scores 1.000 with NO memory. Three explicitly marked
     slots, no distractors and no gap is not a storage challenge -- nothing like MQAR, where four
     pairs among distractors was impossible. So "both" is not storage x tracking; it is tracking
     plus an easy lookup, and whatever makes it hard is not the conjunction I intended to build.
  2. NO BASELINE. Whether "both" is learnable AT ALL at this size is unknown. "The architecture
     cannot compose" and "the task is too hard for anything here" are indistinguishable from this
     table. That is precisely the hole that produced the retracted matrix decision: 170 runs at
     0.32 meant nothing until a transformer scored 1.000 and located the wall.

  conjunctive_baseline.py runs the matched transformer. Until it reports, nothing about composition
  should be claimed in either direction.
