THE CLOCK MATTERS. THE FREQUENCIES DO NOT. BOTH NUMBER-THEORY PREDICTIONS FALSIFIED.
Distance task: random tokens, a marker, a distance k, answer the token k positions back.
seq 48, vocab 32, chance 0.031, 3000 steps, 2 seeds.

  arm         ratio structure       best per seed    k=2    k=5    k=11   k=23   k=43
  none        --                    0.141, 0.219    0.609  0.156  0.089  0.062  0.036
  ---- properly scaled arms -----------------------------------------------------------
  primes      co-prime integers     0.523, 0.539    0.977  0.924  0.612  0.188  0.086
  ratio32     RATIONAL ratio 1.5    0.594, 0.539    0.964  0.951  0.630  0.240  0.081

P1 CONFIRMED, AND IT IS THE RESULT. No clock 0.180, any clock 0.53-0.57. A recurrence gets ORDER
for free -- non-commuting transports are order sensitivity, which is what the group results here
are about -- and gets DISTANCE not at all. A phase rotation on what is written triples performance
on a task that asks how far back something was.

P2 FALSIFIED. golden (phi = 1.618, the most irrational number, strongest protection against Arnold
tongues) 0.547 against ratio32 (rational 1.5) 0.567. The RATIONAL ratio is slightly ahead. Mode
locking and resonance are not operating.

P3 FALSIFIED. primes (co-prime, 2-19) 0.531 against evens (every period shares gcd 2, 2-20) 0.539.
The arm chosen to have the WORST lcm does slightly better. Co-primality is not operating.

All four well-scaled arms are within seed spread of one another. Frequency choice is not a
number-theoretic question.

WHAT ROUND 1'S RANKING ACTUALLY WAS. It looked like none < geometric < powers2 < primes, which fit
the CRT story. Both gaps were scaling artefacts:
  powers2 had a DEAD HEAD -- ang_0 = 2*pi/2^0 is a full turn, i.e. the identity, so that arm ran on
    seven heads while primes ran on eight.
  geometric was MIS-SCALED -- 10000^(-h/8) puts six of eight periods beyond the 48-token sequence,
    so most of its heads barely rotated. That tested RoPE at the wrong scale, not RoPE's choice.
Fix the scaling and the ordering disappears. What remains is: have a clock, and put its periods
inside the sequence length.

THE SEQUENCE OF WRONG MECHANISMS, kept because it is the point.
  1. LCM / Chinese Remainder. Predicted co-prime periods extend unambiguous range, so arms should
     agree at short distances. They did not (0.609 to 0.979 at k=2). Wrong, but it predicted the
     round-1 ranking correctly -- the most dangerous way to be wrong.
  2. Arnold tongues / resonance. Predicted irrational ratios beat rational ones, and explained why
     the effect appears at every distance. Wrong: the rational ratio wins.
  3. What is left is not a mechanism but a constraint: the periods have to fit the sequence.

Two predictions registered in advance, both falsified, and the falsifier said this outcome closes
the line rather than leaving it open. It is closed. Frequency choice is an empirical
hyperparameter with no principle behind it at this scale.

WHAT IS WORTH TAKING FORWARD. Not primes, not phi. The finding that a recurrent state has no
distance sense, and that a cheap phase rotation on the write supplies one. saryu/model.py has no
positional encoding, correctly, because order is free -- and this says distance is a separate thing
we do not have. experimental/memory/saryu_block.py already contains phase_rot, untrained.

NOT ESTABLISHED: no baseline. A transformer with RoPE would likely solve this task outright, and
0.55 would then be a capacity limit rather than a clock limit. Nothing here is about language.
