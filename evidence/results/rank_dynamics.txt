THE ESCAPE IS A LINEAR INSTABILITY, NOT A JUMP
evidence/rank_dynamics.py, 2 pairs, gap 4, AdamW lr 1e-3, 20,000 steps, sampled every 250.

R = M - 1(mean row)^T, where M[i,j] = P(value of pair j | key of pair i). R is EXACTLY zero
for a key-independent readout, so its singular values ARE the key-dependence. At 2 pairs R has
rank at most 1, so sigma1 is the whole story.

PLATEAU, steps 1 to 8250 (34 samples)
  sigma1        mean 0.0047, max 0.0134          -- the noise floor
  accuracy      mean 0.467                       -- the 1/2 rank-one baseline
  diagonal mass mean 0.5007                      -- the exact barycentre
  loss          4.48 -> 0.91

  The loss falls by 80% of its total drop WITH NO KEY-DEPENDENCE WHATSOEVER. Training is
  working the whole time; it is buying the value set and the positional prior.

ESCAPE, steps 8250 to 11000
  log sigma1 is LINEAR in step.  growth rate lambda = 0.00145 per step, R^2 = 0.82
  doubling time 478 steps.  sigma1 0.0048 -> 0.4487

  So the transition is not a discontinuity. It is EXPONENTIAL GROWTH OF AN UNSTABLE MODE from
  the noise floor -- the signature of escaping a saddle along a direction with a positive
  eigenvalue. On a linear axis it looks like a cliff; on a log axis it is a straight line, and
  lambda is the escape eigenvalue.

  This also explains why the plateau LOOKS like nothing is happening. Starting from a noise
  floor of 0.005, exponential growth at lambda needs ~3600 steps to reach 0.1 whatever else is
  going on. The 8250 steps before it are the mode growing from initialisation noise, invisible
  until it clears the floor.

ACCURACY TRACKS KEY-DEPENDENCE, NOT LOSS
  corr(accuracy, sigma1) = +0.948
  corr(accuracy, -loss)  = +0.872
  final: sigma1 0.931, accuracy 0.990, diagonal mass 0.965 -- a vertex, not the barycentre.

VERDICT ON THE REGISTERED PREDICTIONS
  P1 sigma1 flat at ~0 through the plateau then rising sharply   HOLDS, with the correction
     that "sharply" means exponentially, not discontinuously.
  P3 accuracy tracks sigma1 rather than the loss                 HOLDS (+0.948 vs +0.872, and
     the loss fell 4.48 -> 0.91 while accuracy did not move).
  P2 singular values switching on IN SEQUENCE                    UNTESTABLE AT 2 PAIRS, since
     R has rank at most 1 here. It needs 4 pairs, where rank can reach 3, and that run is
     queued. P2 is the one that would explain why more pairs cost more than proportionally.

The falsifier -- sigma1 rising smoothly from step 0 with accuracy following -- did not occur:
sigma1 sat within noise of zero for 8250 steps first.

=============================================================================
4 PAIRS: NO ESCAPE IN 30,000 STEPS

  2 pairs   escape at step ~8,250.  sigma1 -> 0.93, accuracy 0.969, diag mass 0.965
  4 pairs   NO escape in 30,000.    sigma1 max 0.0172 (noise), final 0.0011,
                                    sigma2 max 0.0124, sigma3 max 0.0063,
                                    diag mass 0.2500 exactly, accuracy 0.214
                                    loss still fell 4.51 -> 1.54

So doubling the pairs takes the escape from ~8k steps to more than 30k -- at least 3.6x, and
the run ended without finding it, so the true factor is unknown and larger. The loss fell by
two thirds over those 30,000 steps while the assignment matrix sat at the exact barycentre:
all of that learning is the value set and the positional prior, and none of it is binding.

P2 IS THEREFORE UNTESTED, not confirmed and not refuted. The prediction was that sigma1,
sigma2, sigma3 would switch on IN SEQUENCE with separate plateaus, one saddle per rank. You
cannot observe a sequence of escapes when no escape occurs. Settling it needs a run long
enough to reach the first one, and 30,000 steps is not it.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PROJECT. The binding question is now decided, and the answer is
neither of the two the session spent most of its time on:

  NOT representability   the mechanism works by construction, and at 2 pairs the trained
                         model reaches 0.97 accuracy with a near-permutation assignment.
  NOT architecture       carve, bind-write and the level-2 term are indistinguishable from
                         base across seeds -- they were all measured mid-plateau.
  IT IS ESCAPE TIME      the plateau before binding appears grows steeply with the number of
                         pairs: ~8k at n=2, >30k at n=4. That is the wall, and it is a
                         property of the optimisation, not of the state.

The practical consequence is that every recall experiment in this project that ran for 1,500
to 3,000 steps was measuring the plateau, and none of them could have detected a difference
between architectures even if one existed.
