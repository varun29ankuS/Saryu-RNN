INTEGRALITY OF THE MULTIPLICITIES  (derived from evidence/results/character_table.txt)

A character is a NON-NEGATIVE INTEGER combination of irreps, so how far the measured
multiplicities sit from the nearest integer is a second test of "is this transport a
representation at all" -- one that never looks at the group-law penalty that was used to
decide which runs to score.

  scored as homomorphisms (n=7): worst integrality deviation 0.005
  all other runs          (n=9): best  integrality deviation 0.147
  the two sets are disjoint (0.005 < 0.147), so two independent criteria --
  the group law, and integrality of the irrep multiplicities -- pick out the same runs.

  This matters because the character diagnostic is only meaningful for a transport that
  IS a representation, and integrality tests that without reference to the training
  objective. The runs it rejects are the ones whose <chi,chi> would otherwise be read as
  a quotient label it does not deserve.
