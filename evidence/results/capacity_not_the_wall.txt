evidence/rank_dynamics.py, 4 pairs, gap 4, Muon lr 3e-4, 15,000 steps each.

             max sigma1   final sigma1   diag mass   accuracy   escape (sigma1 > 0.05)

Both sit at the exact barycentre after 15,000 steps, with the optimiser that binds 2 pairs

WHAT THIS ELIMINATES

  the optimiser   Muon is ~10x faster than AdamW at escaping this saddle (2 pairs: step 1,000
                  against 10,000 at matched lr). It does not touch 4 pairs. AdamW also failed
                  4 pairs at 30,000 steps. So the wall is not optimisation.
  n_h, i.e. the   Raising reflections per token from 2 to 8 drops the trace-law overlap from
  trace law's     0.766 to 0.344 -- the prescription's own definition of more binding
  prescription    capacity -- and changes nothing: same barycentre, same accuracy, no escape.

This is the THIRD failure of that prescription in a trained model. The n_h sweep made recall
monotonically WORSE (nh_sweep_shortgap.txt, 0.113 -> 0.004); carving a memory subspace was
within seed noise (multiseed.txt); and now 4x the reflections does nothing at 4 pairs.

The trace law's ARITHMETIC is not in question -- overlap = (1-2/d)^k is verified exactly, and
by construction a k = d/2 store recalls 1.000 at every gap. What has failed three times is
the inference from that arithmetic to "therefore raise n_h in a trained model".

WHAT REMAINS. Something changes between 2 and 4 pairs that is neither optimisation nor
capacity-as-overlap. The next measurement is not another architecture arm: it is
evidence/probe_inside.py, which fits a linear probe at every depth to ask whether the answer
is PRESENT in the state at 4 pairs and merely unreadable, or genuinely absent. Those two
have opposite fixes and nothing measured so far distinguishes them.
