    identity  0.846, 0.829      a fitted linear map, the trivial baseline

The READ printed below declares "P1 HOLDS: inherit (0.3785) beats scratch (0.3804)". That is
a 0.5% difference on per-seed ranges that completely overlap -- scratch spans 0.3804-0.3828,
inherit spans 0.3785-0.3839. There is no effect. The comparison took minima with no noise
floor, which is the THIRD variant in this project of the same mistake: a mean over a bimodal
outcome manufactured a result, a threshold set above an effect size hid one, and now a
difference smaller than the seed spread has been called a win. The script has been fixed to
compare against the within-arm range; this header stands as the record.

WHY INHERITANCE FAILS, and it is specific to this architecture. We L2-normalise q and k, as
the delta-rule family does, which discards the magnitude the teacher's projections encode. And
the delta rule uses k as an ERASE DIRECTION rather than as a similarity key. So a teacher's
attention weights do not mean in this student what they meant in the teacher. The conversion
protocols' students are built to preserve that correspondence. Ours is not.

WHAT SURVIVES. The delta rule accounts for about 62% of the teacher layer's output energy
against the linear baseline's 17%, so it genuinely represents much of what the attention layer
computes. But 0.38 residual is well above the ~0.25 at which layerwise distillation is
reported to recover end-to-end quality after a short finetune.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PLAN. The cheap route -- inherit projections, match layer outputs,
350-700M tokens, ~$100 -- does not apply to us, because the half that makes it cheap is the
inheritance and the inheritance does nothing. Distillation into Saryu would need full
end-to-end training. That is not impossible and may still beat pretraining, but it is not the
bargain RADLADS describes and should not be costed as if it were.

AND THE STANDING CAVEAT, unchanged: what receives any of this is the MATRIX MEMORY, our Gated
DeltaNet equivalent and the component efficiency_hybrid.txt measured as a 2x penalty from
scratch. The Householder transport is not attention-shaped and receives nothing.
