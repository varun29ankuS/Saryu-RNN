THE PRECONDITIONER AMPLIFIES THE ESCAPE, IT DOES NOT CHANGE THE ROUTE
evidence/rank_dynamics.py, 2 pairs, gap 4, lr 3e-4 for BOTH arms, seed 0, sampled every 50.

  step        MUON  ce / acc / sigma1        ADAMW  ce / acc / sigma1
   300      4.034  0.042  0.0107             4.059  0.031  0.0113
   500      2.685  0.214  0.0034             2.918  0.318  0.0063
   750      1.648  0.385  0.0007             1.785  0.432  0.0010
  1000      1.118  0.719  0.3989             1.583  0.458  0.0066
  1500      0.363  1.000  0.9775             1.496  0.474  0.0037
  2500      0.013  0.995  0.9989             1.276  0.542  0.0043

SAME ROUTE. Up to step ~750 the two are indistinguishable in kind: loss falls 4.48 -> ~1.7
while sigma1 sits on the noise floor. Both learn the marginal -- the value set and the
positional prior -- first, and both sit at the rank-one saddle. A hypothesis that muon
"goes at binding directly, with no plateau" was proposed here and is WRONG; muon has a
plateau too, of about 750 steps.

DIFFERENT ESCAPE RATE. What differs is how fast the unstable mode grows once it is moving:

  muon    sigma1 0.0007 -> 0.3989 in 250 steps    lambda ~ 0.025 per step
  adamw   fitted over its escape                  lambda = 0.00145 per step   (17x slower)

  escape step:  muon ~1000,  adamw ~8250 at lr 1e-3 and not at all by 2500 at lr 3e-4.

So the preconditioner does not select a different solution; it multiplies the escape
eigenvalue of the saddle it was always going to leave. That is the conditioning hypothesis,
stated precisely, and it is the first version of it this project has supported.

WHY IT IS PLAUSIBLE. Muon replaces the momentum by the orthogonal factor of its polar
decomposition, so every singular direction of a weight matrix takes an equally sized step.
The unstable direction here is a SMALL singular direction; AdamW's per-coordinate scaling
leaves it under-weighted, while orthogonalisation gives it a full-size step. An earlier note
in this file's neighbourhood argued the opposite -- that equalising would SUPPRESS a mode
that needs scale separation to grow. The data says the reverse.

CAVEATS THAT STILL STAND
  one seed. This project has already produced two single-seed results today that dissolved
    under replication.
  2 pairs only. At 4 pairs muon has not escaped by step 3000 (muon_p4_nh2.txt), so the
    4-pair wall is not merely an optimiser artefact.
  the language model is NOT tested. evidence/results/optimizer_5m.txt compared the two on
    enwik8 and found a tie -- but it swept muon at 0.01/0.02/0.04 with the best at the
    BOTTOM edge, i.e. upward from its optimum, which is the same error made here today. That
    comparison should be redone below 0.01 before any of this is claimed to transfer.
