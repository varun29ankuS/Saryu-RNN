MULTI-SEED REPLICATION: THE ARCHITECTURE DIFFERENCES WERE SEED NOISE
3 arms x 3 seeds, 4 pairs, gap 4, 3000 steps, d=128, 2 layers.
chance top1 = 0.016.  1/pairs = 0.250.

  arm            seeds                  mean     sd     range
  base           0.247, 0.214, 0.242    0.234   0.015   0.034
  carve+bind     0.219, 0.260, 0.198    0.226   0.026   0.063
  sig2           0.237, 0.240, 0.203    0.227   0.017   0.036

  carve+bind vs base   -0.009   0.29x the pooled seed spread   INSIDE NOISE
  sig2       vs base   -0.008   0.35x the pooled seed spread   INSIDE NOISE

WHAT THIS RETRACTS. Earlier in the session, at 1500 steps and ONE seed, carve+bind read
0.184 against base 0.113 and was reported as a 63% improvement. It is not an improvement.
At three seeds and to convergence the three arms are indistinguishable, and the earlier gap
was two artefacts stacked: single-seed noise (spread up to 0.063 within an arm) and scoring
before convergence (base moves 0.195 -> 0.247 between steps 1500 and 3000).

The same applies to every other architecture comparison run at 1500 steps with one seed:
base-wide, carve, sig2-anti. None of them is supported.

WHAT SURVIVES. All three arms converge to ~0.23, just under 1/pairs = 0.250, with in_set
near 0.9. Carving the memory subspace, binding the write, and adding a level-2 term all
land on the same set-membership ceiling. The ceiling is the finding; the arms are not.

THE OPEN PROBLEM THIS LEAVES. sig2 SHOULD have escaped: by construction a level-2 store is
1.000 at every gap and pair count (memory_mechanisms.txt), and linear attention solves MQAR
routinely in the literature. It did not escape here, and the branch was demonstrably alive
(||r_out|| grows from 0 to 2.13; its contribution is 12-18% of the output norm). So either

  the implementation is inadequate  -- it is a readout branch added to the block OUTPUT, not
                                      a second-order term inside the recurrent STATE, and it
                                      has no feature map, or
  learnability, not representability -- the mechanism exists but gradient descent does not
                                      find it at this scale, lr and step budget.

Those are distinguishable and neither is tested yet. What is NOT supported is any claim that
a level-2 term has been shown to help this model.
