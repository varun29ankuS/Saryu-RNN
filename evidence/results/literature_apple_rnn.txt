APPLE IS BETTING ON RNNs, AND I CONCLUDED THE OPPOSITE FROM THE WRONG SOURCE
Checked 2026-09-20.

WHAT I CLAIMED, AND IT WAS WRONG. Reading Apple's neural-processor patents, I wrote: "Apple's ML
silicon IP shows no meaningful bet on recurrence... If they believed recurrent architectures were
the answer to the memory wall, the portfolio would look different. It doesn't. That's evidence
against the direction being where the large players are heading."

THE METHOD WAS THE ERROR, and the user named it: patents lag practice by two to four years -- the
streaming-convolution filing I reasoned from has priority May 2022, before ChatGPT -- so a
portfolio describes what mattered when it was filed. Papers are current. Ask the papers.

WHAT THE PAPERS SAY

  ParaRNN: Unlocking Parallel Training of Nonlinear RNNs for Large Language Models.
  Apple. ICLR 2026, ORAL. Code released: github.com/apple/ml-pararnn

  Apple's own framing: "RNNs are naturally suited to efficient inference, requiring far less memory
  and compute than attention-based architectures, but their sequential nature has historically made
  it impractical to scale up RNNs to billions of parameters."

  THE METHOD. Write the whole sequence as one nonlinear system of equations and apply NEWTON'S
  METHOD. Each iteration linearises the recurrence through its Jacobians, and -- this is the part
  that matters -- "the linearized RNN system has exactly the same form as a linear SSM, with the
  Jacobians playing the role of the state matrices", so an ordinary parallel scan applies.
  Converges in about THREE iterations. 665x over the sequential baseline.

  RESULTS at 7B parameters:
      Mamba2       8.62
      ParaLSTM     9.16
      ParaGRU      9.19
      Transformer  9.55

  A plain LSTM, scaled with a training method that did not exist, beats a transformer on
  perplexity. Linear SSMs still win it.

WHAT THIS CORRECTS IN OUR OWN CODE. saryu/model.py's sequential_nl said "NONLINEAR in the state, so
no parallel scan exists", and that sentence was the stated reason the nonlinear-state path was
treated as a dead end. It is false as an impossibility claim: no DIRECT scan exists, and Newton
linearisation supplies one anyway. The 3.0x/5.4x training cost recorded there is the cost of our
implementation, not a property of nonlinear recurrence. Docstring corrected.

WHAT IT DOES NOT SAY, kept separate from the good news:
  - Perplexity is not recall. Everything this project spent the day on -- associative recall, the
    read, capacity -- is invisible in a perplexity table, and linear models lose specifically there
    (Zoology, Based, and our own MQAR results).
  - Mamba2 still beats both ParaRNN variants, so this is evidence that nonlinear RNNs are
    COMPETITIVE, not that they are the answer.
  - One paper from a research arm is not a company's product bet. The silicon still says vision and
    memory bandwidth.

WHAT IS WORTH DOING WITH IT
  ParaRNN is a TRAINING method and is orthogonal to the read/memory question settled today. It
  would apply to Saryu's nonlinear path if that path is ever wanted, and the code is public. Note
  the shape of the trick is one this project should recognise: linearise, scan, iterate. Our own
  chunk kernels are exact because the recurrence is linear in the state; Newton buys the same
  parallelism for a nonlinear one at the price of a few iterations.
