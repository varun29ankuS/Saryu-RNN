THE WALL IS THE READ. Controlled, with a working reference on both sides.
========================================================================

After 170+ runs and 21 architectural interventions that never moved 4-pair recall off ~0.32, this
file is the controlled comparison that identifies the cause. Two things had to be established
first, and neither existed before today.

1. A BASELINE. The project had never measured the task against anything known to work.

       model         params     2 pairs              4 pairs
       transformer   290,370    1.000 1.000 1.000    1.000 1.000 1.000
       saryu         387,218    1.000 1.000 0.578    ~0.32 (170 runs)

   A SMALLER transformer solves it three times out of three on identical data in the same 5,000
   steps. So the task is solvable at this budget, the wall is architectural, and none of the 21
   nulls were noise around an unreachable target.

2. A WORKING RECURRENT REFERENCE. The first DeltaNet implementation here scored 0.13-0.16, below
   set-restricted chance. That was a bug in my code, not a fact about DeltaNet: it had no short
   causal convolution. arXiv 2609.16183 reports the convolution DOMINATES (~+0.5 recall in both
   model families, with architectural class differences collapsing from +0.32 to +0.03 once both
   have one). I had read that paper hours earlier and not applied it.

       DeltaNet, 4 pairs      conv=False   conv=True
         lr 1e-3                 0.133       0.188
         lr 3e-3                 0.094       0.961

   The convolution takes it from 0.094 to 0.961 at the same learning rate. Published result
   reproduced.

THE CONFOUND, RULED OUT. That result needed lr = 3e-3, and every Saryu run in this project used
3e-4 or 1e-3. Saryu cannot use it:

       saryu, AdamW      lr 1e-3  0.266  (loss 2.540)
                         lr 3e-3  0.102  (loss 4.031, diverged)
                         lr 1e-2  0.023  (loss 4.178, diverged)

   1e-3 is already at its stability limit. Not under-tuning.

THE CONTROLLED RESULT

       transformer                                1.000
       DeltaNet: matrix state + contraction S q   0.961
       Saryu:    vector state + diagonal gate     0.32

   Both recurrent cells have the short conv. Both use Householder-family operators -- Saryu's
   transition IS DeltaProduct's. Learning rate is ruled out in both directions. The isolated
   difference is the MATRIX STATE WITH A CONTRACTING READ.

WHY, AS A DERIVATION RATHER THAN A CORRELATION
   Unrolling gives h_T = a_T T_T [ sum_t M_{t->T-1} b_t ] + b_T. The query sits at position T, so
   its transport T_T appears in every term and FACTORS OUT. A common rotation reorients all stored
   items together; it cannot select one from a sum. The entire query-dependent selection capacity
   reduces to W diag(g_q) T_q, and at nh=2 the transport is within 4% of the identity (tr/dh =
   0.957 on the 25M checkpoint), so selection rests almost entirely on a DIAGONAL gate.

   S q = sum_i v_i (k_i . q) is the item axis that a diagonal gate does not have. Additionally
   DeltaNet's transition (I - beta k k^T) is RANK ONE -- it erases one direction and leaves every
   item with an orthogonal key untouched -- where Saryu rotates the entire state on every token.
   Same operator family, applied targeted rather than globally. That is also the tracking/memory
   antagonism this project measured empirically and mistook for a law.

WHAT THIS RETIRES
   Not the read form as a tuning knob, not the binding operator, not the geometry, not the schedule.
   Twenty-one interventions all modified the transport, the write, or the training order. None
   touched the thing that was wrong.

   Sign binding, the cheapest possible operator (exact involution, O(d), capacity matching
   convolution at a tenth the cost), trained WORSE than its control: 0.258/0.242 against
   0.289-0.336. Consistent with this diagnosis -- a better binding does nothing when the read
   cannot select.

WHAT TO DO
   Adopt the matrix state and the contracting read. This is what Qwen3-Next (36 Gated DeltaNet + 12
   Gated Attention, 3:1) and Kimi Linear (20 KDA + 7 MLA) ship in production. Saryu's Householder
   product transition is DeltaProduct's and is retained; what changes is the state shape and the
   read.

OPEN / NOT CLAIMED
   - One seed per cell in the debug sweep; the 0.961 needs replication across seeds.
   - The wide-vector-beats-matrix construction (0.993 vs 0.527 at equal floats, 128 pairs) is NOT
     refuted -- the hrr cell here was broken, stuck at chance loss for 5,000 steps because the
     unitary normalisation x/|x| annihilates the magnitude gradient and passes phase only. The
     vector route is untested, not dead.
   - Whether a vector state with a proper unbinding read can match the matrix at equal floats
     remains the one claim this project could still own, and it needs a working implementation.
