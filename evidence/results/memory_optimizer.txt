P1 NOT SUPPORTED. Muon at 3e-4 is worse on every measure. Muon at 1e-3 has the single best score
seen (0.977 against AdamW's best of 0.945) but that is one seed.

P2 UNRESOLVED, AND MY FIRST READING OF IT WAS PREMATURE. I wrote that P2 was "falsified in the
opposite direction" from the muon@3e-4 arm while the 1e-3 arm was still incomplete. With both of
its seeds finished the picture is different and thinner than either claim:

    adamw@1e-3   0.797, 0.945, 0.922   spread 0.148
    muon@3e-4    0.312, 0.781, 0.867   spread 0.555
    muon@1e-3    0.977, 0.945          spread 0.031    <- tightest in the sweep

At the WRONG rate Muon is much worse and much wider. At 1e-3 it has the tightest spread and the
highest scores. But AdamW's 0.148 comes entirely from one bad seed -- its other two are 0.945 and
0.922, a spread of 0.023, TIGHTER than Muon's 0.031. So on two or three seeds the honest
difference is solve rate, 2/2 for Muon@1e-3 against 2/3 for AdamW, which is weak evidence either
way. P2 is not settled and should not be quoted in either direction.

P3 NOT SUPPORTED, AND THIS IS THE RESULT THAT IS ACTUALLY SETTLED. Steps to 0.8 is 2250 under
EVERY optimizer and rate that reached it -- adamw@1e-3 on two seeds, muon@1e-3 on both. Unchanged
from the value that produced the 1.8x excess over a faithful DeltaNet (750 transformer, 1250
DeltaNet, 2250 Saryu+memory). The optimizer does not move convergence speed at all.

SO THE EXCESS IS NOT AN OPTIMIZER ARTEFACT. That much is firm: 2250 steps regardless. It stays
architectural until something else explains it.

THE BIMODALITY IS STILL OPEN. It was reported here as "not an optimizer artefact either", which
the completed data does not support -- muon@1e-3 solved 2/2 where adamw solved 2/3, and the
sample is far too small to separate that from chance. Neither claimed.

WHY THE PREDICTION WAS WRONG, AND IT IS A SCOPE ERROR. optimizer_escape.txt measured Muon beating
AdamW 1.000 to 0.474 on 2-pair MQAR -- but on the TRANSPORT-ONLY model, with no matrix memory.
That was generalised here to a different architecture on a harder task. The advantage was real
where measured and does not transfer.

This is the same mistake as the standing "keep AdamW" verdict, which came from optimizer_5m on
LANGUAGE MODELLING and was carried into recall experiments unexamined. An optimizer result is
scoped to the architecture AND the task it was measured on. Both directions of that error were
made in one day.

WHAT THIS LEAVES. One useful practical finding: Muon at 3e-4 is a bad setting for this
configuration, which matters because gate_retention.py uses exactly that -- Muon at LR=3e-4. The
gate-bound result (6/7 against 1/3) was obtained under an optimizer setting shown here to be poor
for the memory. That does not invalidate it, since it is a comparison between two arms sharing the
setting, but it means the absolute numbers there are not a ceiling.

INCOMPLETE. muon@1e-3 has one finished seed of three; the machine ran out of memory twice during
this sweep. The conclusion above rests on the three complete seeds of adamw@1e-3 and muon@3e-4 and
should be revisited if muon@1e-3 is ever finished.
